Thumbstone

You are scrolling your life away. Nobody is stopping you.

Do the math

The average person spends 2 hours and 21 minutes a day on social media.1

That's more than a month every year, around the clock. Over a lifetime, it adds up to almost eight of your fifty-three waking years.

Eight years of your one life, spent on a feed that will never remember you.

1 Average daily time spent on social media worldwide, February 2025. Statista. Lifetime figures assume an 80-year life with 16 waking hours a day.

The quiet trade

Every day, we trade small pieces of our lives for something we won't remember by tonight. No one forces us. The trade is just so easy that we stopped noticing we were making it.

The feed was built by some of the smartest people alive to make that trade feel free. Thumbstone puts a small price on it again, so you can decide if it's worth it.

No need to go cold turkey

You don't have to delete everything or buy a flip phone. Your phone is how you talk to friends, find your way and get through the day.

Thumbstone is for you if you want to keep your apps, just not let them keep you. You can still open anything you want. It just costs a little more every time.

Only the time that matters

Your phone's screen time counts everything: maps, messages, photos, the notes from your lecture, the call with your mom. The number gets big, and most of it isn't the problem.

Thumbstone only counts the apps you choose, the ones built to keep you scrolling. That's dead time. Not the time you spent on your phone, but the time you'd actually want back.

How it works

Choose what eats your day. Social media, short videos, the news. The apps you pick stay locked.

Type to unlock. To open one, you type a short text by hand, a quiet reminder of what your time is for. The first one of the day is ten words. Each unlock after that is five words longer, up to fifty. Then you get fifteen minutes.

Every minute is counted. Thumbstone shows your dead time today, over the last thirty days, and what it adds up to over your life.

Stay under your limit to stay alive. Every day under your daily limit keeps your streak alive. Go over, and the day gets a grave in your graveyard. You can bring it back the next day, but only if you earn it.
