Common questions

I tapped "Type to unlock" but nothing happened.

Thumbstone sends a notification. Tap it to open the typing screen. Make sure notifications are turned on for Thumbstone in Settings.

The app locked again before I used it.

An unlock gives you the next 15 minutes, whether you use the app or not.

iOS locks the app even after I typed my text.

Turn off App Limits and Downtime for the same apps in Settings > Screen Time. Keep Screen Time itself turned on.
